Streaming 6-DoF relative poses for every neighbour, learned in simulation and deployed zero-shot. 端到端流式输出全部邻居的 6-DoF 相对位姿，仿真训练、零样本部署。

Abstract 摘要

A robot team can only cooperate if every robot knows where its teammates are. Classical relative state estimators solve that problem with a hand-crafted optimization problem that has to be re-derived and re-tuned whenever the sensor suite changes. NeuRIO learns the estimator instead. Every robot state becomes a token of a graph and every mutual bearing / distance observation becomes an edge; a gravity alignment step removes most of the sensitivity to how the sensors are mounted, the edge information modulates the attention, and token states and edge states are refreshed alternately so that the network streams the 6-DoF relative poses of all neighbours end to end. The model is trained on roughly 2,000 minutes of simulated trajectories and is deployed on real robots zero-shot with frozen weights, with an optional label-free LoRA adaptation that keeps improving it online. On real swarm sequences it reaches 0.12 m / 2.5° on average, gains another 5% after adaptation, and keeps up real-time streaming inference at the scale of 200 robots.

多机器人系统只有知道队友在哪里才能真正协同。传统相对状态估计把这一问题建模为手工设计的优化问题，每当传感器配置变化就需要重新推导和调参。NeuRIO 选择直接学习这个估计器：把每个机器人的状态作为图中的 token、把机间的方位/距离观测作为边，先通过重力对齐降低对传感器安装差异的敏感性，再将边信息加入 Attention 进行调制，并交替更新 token 与边状态，端到端流式输出全部邻居的 6-DoF 相对位姿。模型使用约 2,000 分钟仿真轨迹训练，冻结权重即可零样本部署，并支持无标签 LoRA 在线自适应微调。真机集群序列平均达到 0.12 m / 2.5°，微调后平均误差进一步降低 5%，并实现了 200 台机器人规模下的实时流式推理。

Highlights 技术亮点

  • Physics-guided graph representation. Robot states are tokens, mutual bearing and distance observations are edges, and a gravity alignment step makes the estimator far less sensitive to how the sensors are mounted on each platform.
  • Edge-state attention. Edge information modulates the attention while token and edge states are updated alternately, which lets the network output the 6-DoF relative poses of every neighbour end to end and in a streaming fashion.
  • Zero-shot sim-to-real. About 2,000 minutes of simulated trajectories are enough to deploy with frozen weights; a label-free LoRA adaptation refines the model online without any ground truth.
  • Accuracy and scale on real robots. 0.12 m / 2.5° on average over real swarm sequences, a further 5% error reduction after adaptation, and real-time streaming inference with 200 robots.
  • 物理引导的图表示。以每个机器人状态为 token、机间方位/距离观测为边，并通过重力对齐降低估计器对传感器安装差异的敏感性。
  • 边状态调制的注意力。把边信息加入 Attention 进行调制，交替更新 token 与边状态，端到端流式输出全部邻居的 6-DoF 相对位姿。
  • 零样本 Sim-to-Real。约 2,000 分钟仿真轨迹训练后，冻结权重即可直接部署；并支持无需真值的无标签 LoRA 在线自适应微调。
  • 真机精度与规模。真机集群序列平均 0.12 m / 2.5°，微调后误差再降低 5%，可在 200 台机器人规模下实时流式推理。
