Abstract 摘要

Ground vehicles are cheap, but the sensors that let them see obstacles are not. ColAG asks what happens if the ground vehicles carry no environment-perception sensors at all and borrow the eyes of a UAV instead. Each UGV predicts the potential collisions implied by its own state uncertainty, and the UAV automatically schedules and plans flights to cover the UGVs that need support most. In real-world experiments a single UAV guided three UGVs without any environment-perception sensors to their goals with zero collisions. The same line of work also produced a cost-effective swarm navigation system that raises the success rate in concave-obstacle scenes from 30% to 90% with FOV-limited cameras, the CREPES relative localization system (0.10 m / 0.73° median error between two robots), CoNi-MPC cooperative control in the non-inertial relative frame, and a PPO-based end-to-end policy that tracks a reference robot from relative observations alone, without any communication.

无人车本身很便宜，但让它“看见”障碍物的传感器并不便宜。ColAG 的问题是：如果无人车完全不搭载环境感知传感器，转而借用无人机的眼睛，会发生什么。每台无人车预测自身状态不确定性可能导致的潜在碰撞，无人机则自动调度与规划航线，去支援最需要帮助的无人车。真机实验中，1 架无人机引导 3 台未搭载环境感知传感器的无人车零碰撞到达目标。同一研究线还包括：低成本集群导航系统，在使用 FOV 受限相机的条件下把凹形障碍场景的导航成功率从 30% 提升到 90%；CREPES 相对定位系统，双机定位中位误差 0.10 m / 0.73°；CoNi-MPC 在非惯性相对坐标系下的协同控制；以及基于 PPO 的端到端策略，仅凭相对观测预测参考机 IMU 状态，无需通信即可自主跟踪。

Highlights 技术亮点

  • Uncertainty-driven cooperation. The UGVs predict the collisions their own state uncertainty may cause, and the UAV is scheduled and planned automatically to support them.
  • Zero-collision hardware demonstration. One UAV guided three UGVs carrying no environment-perception sensors to their goals without a single collision.
  • Cost-effective swarm navigation. A companion system raises the navigation success rate in concave-obstacle scenes from 30% to 90% while using FOV-limited cameras.
  • The supporting stack. CREPES relative localization (0.10 m / 0.73° median error between two robots), CoNi-MPC cooperative control in the non-inertial relative frame, and a PPO policy that tracks a reference robot from relative observations only.
  • 不确定性驱动的协同。无人车预测自身不确定性导致的潜在碰撞，无人机自动调度与规划以支援无人车。
  • 真机零碰撞演示。1 架无人机引导 3 台未搭载环境感知传感器的无人车零碰撞到达目标。
  • 低成本集群导航。配套系统在使用 FOV 受限相机的同时，把凹形障碍场景导航成功率由 30% 提升至 90%。
  • 支撑技术栈。CREPES 相对定位（双机中位误差 0.10 m / 0.73°）、CoNi-MPC 非惯性相对坐标系协同控制，以及仅凭相对观测跟踪参考机的 PPO 端到端策略。
