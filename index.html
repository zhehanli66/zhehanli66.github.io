I am a Ph.D. student in Control Science and Engineering at the FAST Lab (Fire Group), Zhejiang University, advised by Yanjun Cao. I received my B.Eng. in Mechatronics and Automation from the Chu Kochen Honors College, Zhejiang University, in 2022, and I expect to receive my Ph.D. in June 2027.

I work on making a team of robots know where they are with respect to each other, and on turning that capability into cooperative behaviour in the real world. My work spans the full stack — custom sensing hardware, estimation, planning, control and decision making — and is consistently validated on real multi-robot systems rather than in simulation alone.

Research Interests

  • Multi-robot relative localization and state estimation. Bearing–distance–inertial fusion, continuous-time estimation, outlier-robust optimization, learning-based and zero-shot estimators.
  • Cooperative perception and active sensing. Air–ground collaboration, active 3D reconstruction, gimbal planning, online multi-sensor calibration.
  • Full-stack multi-robot systems. Custom localization hardware, VIO/LIO, trajectory planning, MPC, sim-to-real and reinforcement learning, real-robot swarm deployment.

NeuRIO

NeuRIO: Physics-Guided Edge-State Graph Learning for Zero-Shot Multi-Robot Relative Inertial OdometryNeuRIO：面向多机器人零样本相对惯性里程计的物理引导边状态图学习

Zhehan Li, …

Full author list will be released with the preprint.完整作者列表将随预印本一同公开。

Under review在投 First author第一作者

Every robot state becomes a token and every mutual bearing/range observation an edge; node and edge states are updated alternately so one graph network streams 6-DoF relative poses for all neighbours at once. Trained on simulated trajectories only and deployed zero-shot with frozen weights: 0.12 m / 2.5° on real swarm sequences, still real time at 200 robots.以每个机器人的状态为 token、机间方位/距离观测为边，交替更新节点与边状态，由一张图网络端到端流式输出全部邻居的 6-DoF 相对位姿。仅用仿真轨迹训练、冻结权重零样本部署，真机集群序列平均 0.12 m / 2.5°，200 台规模下仍能实时推理。

CREPES-X

CREPES-X: Hierarchical Bearing-Distance-Inertial Direct Cooperative Relative Pose Estimation SystemCREPES-X：多层次直接式的协作多机器人相对位姿估计系统

Zhehan Li, Zheng Wang, Jiadong Lu, Qi Liu, Zhiren Xun, Yue Wang, Fei Gao, Chao Xu, Yanjun Cao

IEEE Transactions on Robotics (major revision)IEEE Transactions on Robotics（二审） First author第一作者

An integrated infrared-fisheye / UWB / IMU relative-localization system for GPS-denied, non-line-of-sight and high-speed cooperation: closed-form single-frame initialization, multi-frame optimization tightly coupled with inter-robot IMU pre-integration, and an angular-consistency outlier rejection that keeps 96.8% precision at a 90% outlier rate. The hardware is 77% smaller and 50% lighter than the previous generation; real-robot RMSE is 0.073 m / 1.8° with 20 robots running live.面向 GPS 拒止、非视距与高速协同场景的红外鱼眼 / UWB / IMU 一体化相对定位系统：单帧闭式初始化、紧耦合多机 IMU 预积分的多帧优化，以及基于角度一致性的高外点率剔除算法（90% 外点率下精确率 96.8%）。硬件较前代体积缩小 77%、重量降低 50%，真机 RMSE 0.073 m / 1.8°，支持 20 台机器人实时运行。

CT-RIO

Parallel Continuous-Time Relative Localization with Augmented Clamped Non-Uniform B-Splines基于增广钳制非均匀 B 样条的并行连续时间相对定位

Jiadong Lu*, Zhehan Li*, Tao Han, Miao Xu, Chao Xu, Yanjun Cao

*Equal contribution.*共同第一作者。

IEEE Transactions on Robotics (major revision)IEEE Transactions on Robotics（二审） Co-first author共同第一作者

A continuous-time factor graph on augmented clamped non-uniform B-splines lets asynchronous, differently-rated bearing / range / IMU measurements be fused at their raw timestamps, adapts the knot density to how aggressive the motion is, and estimates inter-robot clock offsets online (hundreds of milliseconds converge to sub-millisecond within 3 s). An incremental asynchronous block-coordinate-descent solver, proven to converge, keeps it real time: 0.046 m / 1.8° RMSE, up to 60% better than discrete-time optimization on fast sequences.基于增广钳制非均匀 B 样条的连续时间因子图，使异步、异频的方位/距离/IMU 观测能按原始时间戳融合，并自适应运动剧烈程度、在线估计机间时钟偏移（数百毫秒初始时偏可在 3 s 内收敛至亚毫秒）。配合可证明收敛的增量异步块坐标下降并行求解，真机 RMSE 0.046 m / 1.8°，高速序列较离散时间优化最高降低 60%。

AIOG

To See All: LiDAR-Guided Active Reconstruction via Online Calibration and Gimbal PlanningTo See All：基于在线标定与云台规划的激光引导主动三维重建

Anonymous Author*, Zhehan Li*, …

*Co-first author. Full author list will be released with the preprint.*共同第一作者，完整作者列表将随预印本一同公开。

Under review在投 Co-first author共同第一作者

LiDAR-guided active reconstruction built from three pieces: an online camera-LiDAR extrinsic network that fuses RGB and projected-depth features by cross-modal attention and refines the 6-DoF residual in cascaded stages, a budgeted orienteering formulation of gimbal colorization solved with MINCO trajectory optimization, and an SAC policy that reaches 6.0% more color coverage using only 51% of the gimbal travel. An extrinsic-calibration dataset (32 scenes, 50k+ hardware-synchronized image-cloud pairs) is released with it.激光引导的主动三维重建，由三部分组成：在线相机-激光外参自标定网络（跨模态注意力融合 RGB 与投影深度特征，多阶段级联回归 6-DoF 外参残差）、以带奖励衰减的预算 Orienteering 建模并用 MINCO 轨迹优化求解的云台着色规划，以及端到端 SAC 策略（仅用 51% 的云台行程，着色覆盖率提升 6.0%）。同时开源 32 个场景、5 万余对硬件同步图像-点云的外参标定数据集。

The first-generation cooperative relative pose estimation system — active IR LEDs, an IR fisheye camera, UWB and an IMU — in which IR light communication solves the association between visual detections and range measurements, giving 6-DoF relative pose from a single frame of measurements at 0.10 m / 0.73° median error. Industry collaboration企业合作项目 Project lead项目负责人 Zhejiang University · September 2025 – September 2026浙江大学 · 2025 年 9 月 – 2026 年 9 月

An autonomous inspection system that uses a single RGB-D camera on a micro UAV, built on ORB-SLAM3, EGO-Planner and PX4 running on a Jetson Orin NX. It covers the whole loop — offline mapping, waypoint teaching, cross-session relocalization, obstacle avoidance and oriented photo capture — with visual-pose delay compensation, SLAM-loss protection, dynamic local-map updates and hot waypoint reloading. I lead requirement breakdown, scheduling and delivery, and drove one patent application.仅用单颗 RGB-D 相机的微型无人机自主巡检系统，基于 ORB-SLAM3、EGO-Planner 与 PX4 在 Jetson Orin NX 上二次开发，打通离线建图、航点示教、跨会话重定位、自主避障与定向拍照全流程，并实现视觉位姿延迟补偿、SLAM 跟丢保护、局部地图动态更新与航点热加载。我负责需求拆解、进度管理、校企沟通与成果汇报，并主导撰写专利申请 1 项。
