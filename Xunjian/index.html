Abstract 摘要

Power distribution rooms are narrow, GPS-denied and full of equipment that has to be photographed from the same viewpoint every time. This industry project builds a micro-UAV that inspects them autonomously using a single RGB-D camera. I led the requirement breakdown, the schedule, the communication with the industry partner and the reporting of results, and led one patent application. The system is built on ORB-SLAM3, EGO-Planner and PX4 and runs on a Jetson Orin NX. The full workflow is closed: offline mapping, waypoint teaching, cross-session relocalization, autonomous obstacle avoidance and targeted photo capture. Visual pose latency compensation, SLAM tracking-loss protection, dynamic local map updates and hot-loading of waypoints keep the localization–planning–control chain reliable in tight GPS-denied spaces.

Highlights 技术亮点

  • One camera is enough. A single RGB-D camera on a Jetson Orin NX, with ORB-SLAM3, EGO-Planner and PX4 developed further for the task.
  • A closed inspection workflow. Offline mapping, waypoint teaching, cross-session relocalization, autonomous obstacle avoidance and targeted photo capture.
  • Reliability engineering. Visual pose latency compensation, SLAM tracking-loss protection, dynamic local map updates and waypoint hot-loading for tight GPS-denied spaces.
  • Project leadership. Requirement breakdown, schedule management, university–industry communication and reporting, plus one patent application.
  • 单相机方案。在 Jetson Orin NX 上仅使用单颗 RGB-D 相机，基于 ORB-SLAM3、EGO-Planner 与 PX4 二次开发。
  • 完整巡检流程闭环。离线建图、航点示教、跨会话重定位、自主避障与定向拍照全流程打通。
  • 可靠性工程。视觉位姿延迟补偿、SLAM 跟丢保护、局部地图动态更新与航点热加载，保障无 GPS 狭小环境下的链路可靠性。
