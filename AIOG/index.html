Overview 项目概述

A wide-field-of-view LiDAR observes scene geometry beyond the camera frustum, leaving many measured surfaces without color or texture. This project records those appearance gaps in the LiDAR map and actively points a gimbal camera toward them. The gimbal encoder supplies an immediate camera pose for planning; the Tri-Axis Principal-Alignment Network then corrects its residual 6-DoF error from the RGB image and projected LiDAR depth for accurate reconstruction. The MINCO planner maintains a voxelized appearance-coverage frontier, orders useful yaw–pitch targets as a motion-budgeted Orienteering Problem with arrival-time reward decay, and refines the result into a smooth trajectory. The accompanying COOKIE dataset provides 50,006 hardware-synchronized image–scan pairs from 20 sequences and 32 scenes. Evaluation separates calibration, observation coverage, colored-point-cloud quality and downstream 3D Gaussian Splatting quality.

大视场激光雷达能够观测相机视锥以外的场景几何，因此许多已测表面仍缺少颜色或纹理信息。本项目在激光地图中显式记录这些外观覆盖空缺，并主动控制云台相机补充图像观测。规划阶段直接采用云台编码器给出的即时相机位姿；重建阶段则由 Tri-Axis Principal-Alignment Network 融合 RGB 与投影深度，校正编码器外参中的 6-DoF 残差。MINCO 规划模块维护体素化外观覆盖前沿，将有效的偏航—俯仰视角排序建模为带运动预算和到达时间奖励衰减的 Orienteering Problem，并进一步优化为平滑轨迹。配套 COOKIE 数据集包含 20 条序列、32 个场景和 50,006 对硬件同步图像—扫描数据；评估分别考察标定精度、观测覆盖、彩色点云质量和下游 3D Gaussian Splatting 质量。

Key Contributions 主要贡献

  • Online residual calibration. Principal-point-anchored horizontal, vertical and polar sampling exposes axis-specific misalignment cues; cross-modal attention and cascaded re-projection jointly refine the 6-DoF camera–LiDAR residual.
  • Coverage-aware gimbal planning. The MINCO planner turns camera-unobserved LiDAR surfaces into a utility field, orders views under a motion budget and uses receding-horizon replanning as the carrier moves.
  • Smooth, executable trajectories. MINCO-based refinement converts discrete yaw–pitch targets into continuous gimbal commands.
  • Dataset and end-to-end evaluation. COOKIE contains 50,006 synchronized pairs across 32 scenes, and the evaluation traces improvements from calibration and image coverage through colored point clouds and 3DGS reconstruction.
  • 在线残差标定。围绕图像主点进行水平、竖直和极坐标采样以提取轴向错位线索，再通过跨模态注意力与级联重投影联合细化 6-DoF 相机—激光外参残差。
  • 覆盖感知的云台规划。MINCO 规划模块将尚未被相机观测的激光表面转化为效用场，在运动预算内排序视角，并随载体运动进行滚动重规划。
  • 平滑可执行的轨迹。基于 MINCO 的连续优化将离散偏航—俯仰目标转化为可执行的云台指令。
  • 数据集与端到端评估。COOKIE 覆盖 32 个场景、包含 50,006 对同步数据；评估从标定与图像覆盖一直延伸到彩色点云和 3DGS 重建质量。

Future Work 后续工作

MINCO planning selects viewpoints with an explicit utility function and then optimizes a smooth gimbal trajectory. In ongoing work, we are studying a learned alternative: we formulate gimbal control as a continuous-control problem and train a Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) policy. At each 0.1 s decision step, the policy outputs yaw and pitch angular accelerations and receives the number of newly colored surface voxels as its reward.

MINCO 规划先根据显式设计的效用函数选择视角，再优化平滑的云台轨迹。在后续工作中，我们尝试另一条思路：将云台控制建模为连续控制问题，并训练一个 Soft Actor-Critic（SAC）策略。策略每 0.1 秒输出偏航和俯仰两轴的角加速度，以该时间步内新增的着色体素数作为奖励。

To account for vehicle motion and gimbal response, the policy observes visibility-gain maps for the present and 1, 2 and 4 s into the future. For each candidate yaw–pitch direction, a map records how many currently known, uncolored voxels could be observed if that direction were maintained over the corresponding horizon. The state also includes the carrier’s predicted displacement and the remaining flight time. The replay below compares the SAC policy with MINCO planning on the same recorded 60 s flight. In this example, the SAC policy reaches 64.2% coverage with 2,468° of cumulative gimbal rotation; MINCO planning reaches 58.1% with 4,821°.

为使策略能够考虑载体运动和云台响应，观测中加入当前及未来 1、2、4 秒的可见性增益图。对每个偏航–俯仰候选方向，图中记录的是：若在相应时间范围内保持该方向，当前地图中有多少尚未着色的已知体素可被相机观测。状态还包含载体的预测位移和剩余航程。下面的可视化在同一段 60 秒录制航迹上回放 SAC 策略与 MINCO 规划。在这个案例中，SAC 策略以 2,468° 的累计云台转角达到 64.2% 的着色覆盖率；MINCO 规划则以 4,821° 达到 58.1%。
