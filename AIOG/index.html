Abstract 摘要

Colouring a LiDAR map with a gimbal camera fails in two ways: the extrinsics drift, and the camera simply never looks at large parts of the scene. This project attacks both. An online self-calibration network extracts pitch, yaw and roll misalignment patterns separately, fuses RGB features with projected depth features through cross-modal self- and cross-attention, and jointly regresses the 6-DoF extrinsic residual; a multi-stage cascade of extrinsic update, depth re-projection and residual re-estimation drives it to convergence. The work also releases COOKIE, a calibration dataset of 32 scenes and more than 50,000 hardware-synchronized image–point-cloud pairs. On top of a visibility and colouring state map, active gimbal colouring is formulated as a budgeted orienteering problem with reward decay and solved together with MINCO trajectory optimization for smooth gimbal motion, and evaluated with a multi-level protocol that measures both point-domain colour and downstream 3DGS rendering quality. Finally, the same viewpoint control problem is cast as continuous control — visibility gain map as observation, dual-axis angular acceleration as action, coloured-voxel increment as reward — and solved end to end with SAC, which improves colouring coverage by 6.0% while using only 51% of the gimbal travel of the planning baseline.

用云台相机为激光地图着色会在两个环节失败：外参会漂移，相机也根本看不到场景中的大片区域。本工作同时解决这两点。在线外参自标定网络分别提取俯仰、偏航与横滚的错位模式，经跨模态自注意力与交叉注意力融合 RGB 与投影深度特征，联合回归 6-DoF 外参残差，并通过多阶段“外参更新—深度重投影—残差再估计”级联逐步收敛；同时构建并开源外参标定数据集 COOKIE（32 个场景、5 万余对硬件同步图像—点云）。在可见性与着色状态地图之上，将云台主动着色建模为带奖励衰减的预算 Orienteering 问题，结合 MINCO 轨迹优化生成平滑云台运动，并提出覆盖点域颜色与下游 3DGS 渲染质量的多层级评估协议。进一步将云台视角控制建模为连续控制问题：以可见性增益图为观测、双轴角加速度为动作、着色体素增量为奖励，使用 SAC 训练端到端策略，相较规划方法仅用 51% 的云台行程即提升 6.0% 的着色覆盖率。

Highlights 技术亮点

  • Online extrinsic self-calibration. Pitch, yaw and roll misalignment patterns are extracted separately and fused with cross-modal self- and cross-attention over RGB and projected depth features, then refined by a multi-stage update / re-projection / re-estimation cascade.
  • The COOKIE dataset. 32 scenes and more than 50,000 hardware-synchronized image–point-cloud pairs, released for camera–LiDAR extrinsic calibration.
  • Gimbal planning as budgeted orienteering. A visibility and colouring state map turns active colouring into an orienteering problem with reward decay, solved together with MINCO trajectory optimization for smooth gimbal motion.
  • A learned colourization policy. Visibility gain map as observation, dual-axis angular acceleration as action and coloured-voxel increment as reward: a SAC policy improves coverage by 6.0% while using 51% of the gimbal travel.
  • Multi-level evaluation. Both point-domain colour quality and downstream 3D Gaussian splatting rendering quality are measured.
  • 在线外参自标定。分别提取俯仰、偏航与横滚错位模式，经跨模态自/交叉注意力融合 RGB 与投影深度特征，并通过多阶段“外参更新—深度重投影—残差再估计”级联逐步收敛。
  • COOKIE 数据集。构建并开源相机—激光外参标定数据集，包含 32 个场景、5 万余对硬件同步图像—点云。
  • 云台规划建模为预算 Orienteering。基于可见性与着色状态地图，把主动着色建模为带奖励衰减的 Orienteering 问题，并结合 MINCO 轨迹优化生成平滑云台运动。
  • 强化学习着色策略。以可见性增益图为观测、双轴角加速度为动作、着色体素增量为奖励，SAC 训练的端到端策略仅用 51% 云台行程提升 6.0% 着色覆盖率。
  • 多层级评估协议。同时评估点域颜色质量与下游 3DGS 渲染质量。
