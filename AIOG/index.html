Overview 项目概述

A wide-field-of-view LiDAR observes scene geometry beyond the camera frustum, leaving many measured surfaces without color or texture. This project records those appearance gaps in the LiDAR map and actively points a gimbal camera toward them. The gimbal encoder supplies an immediate camera pose for planning; the Tri-Axis Principal-Alignment Network then corrects its residual 6-DoF error from the RGB image and projected LiDAR depth for accurate reconstruction. SECP maintains a voxelized appearance-coverage frontier, orders useful yaw–pitch targets as a motion-budgeted Orienteering Problem with arrival-time reward decay, and refines the result into a smooth trajectory with MINCO. The accompanying COOKIE dataset provides 50,006 hardware-synchronized image–scan pairs from 20 sequences and 32 scenes. Evaluation separates calibration, observation coverage, colored-point-cloud quality and downstream 3D Gaussian Splatting quality.

大视场激光雷达能够观测相机视锥以外的场景几何，因此许多已测表面仍缺少颜色或纹理信息。本项目在激光地图中显式记录这些外观覆盖空缺，并主动控制云台相机补充图像观测。规划阶段直接采用云台编码器给出的即时相机位姿；重建阶段则由 Tri-Axis Principal-Alignment Network 融合 RGB 与投影深度，校正编码器外参中的 6-DoF 残差。SECP 维护体素化外观覆盖前沿，将有效的偏航—俯仰视角排序建模为带运动预算和到达时间奖励衰减的 Orienteering Problem，再利用 MINCO 优化为平滑轨迹。配套 COOKIE 数据集包含 20 条序列、32 个场景和 50,006 对硬件同步图像—扫描数据；评估分别考察标定精度、观测覆盖、彩色点云质量和下游 3D Gaussian Splatting 质量。

Key Contributions 主要贡献

  • Online residual calibration. Principal-point-anchored horizontal, vertical and polar sampling exposes axis-specific misalignment cues; cross-modal attention and cascaded re-projection jointly refine the 6-DoF camera–LiDAR residual.
  • Coverage-aware gimbal planning. SECP turns camera-unobserved LiDAR surfaces into a utility field, orders views under a motion budget and uses receding-horizon replanning as the carrier moves.
  • Smooth, executable trajectories. MINCO-based refinement converts discrete yaw–pitch targets into continuous gimbal commands.
  • Dataset and end-to-end evaluation. COOKIE contains 50,006 synchronized pairs across 32 scenes, and the evaluation traces improvements from calibration and image coverage through colored point clouds and 3DGS reconstruction.
  • 在线残差标定。围绕图像主点进行水平、竖直和极坐标采样以提取轴向错位线索，再通过跨模态注意力与级联重投影联合细化 6-DoF 相机—激光外参残差。
  • 覆盖感知的云台规划。SECP 将尚未被相机观测的激光表面转化为效用场，在运动预算内排序视角，并随载体运动进行滚动重规划。
  • 平滑可执行的轨迹。基于 MINCO 的连续优化将离散偏航—俯仰目标转化为可执行的云台指令。
  • 数据集与端到端评估。COOKIE 覆盖 32 个场景、包含 50,006 对同步数据；评估从标定与图像覆盖一直延伸到彩色点云和 3DGS 重建质量。
