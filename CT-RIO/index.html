Asynchronous bearing, distance and inertial measurements, fused at their raw timestamps on a continuous-time spline. 在连续时间样条上，按原始时间戳融合异步的方位、距离与惯性测量。

Abstract 摘要

Relative localization data arrives asynchronously and at very different rates: bearing, distance and inertial measurements never share a common clock, and forcing them onto discretized states throws away accuracy and adds latency. This work builds a continuous-time factor graph on clamped non-uniform B-splines, so every measurement is fused at its own raw timestamp, the knot distribution adapts to how aggressive the motion is, and the inter-robot clock offset is estimated online — an initial offset of hundreds of milliseconds converges to the sub-millisecond level within 3 s. Closed-form extension and deletion rules for clamped B-splines let the trajectory grow and shrink without deforming the curve, and an incremental asynchronous block coordinate descent, for which convergence is proven, solves the problem in parallel and fast enough to sit inside a UAV control loop. Real-world experiments reach 0.046 m / 1.8° RMSE, up to 60% better than discrete-time optimization on high-speed sequences.

相对定位的观测天然是异步、异频的：方位、距离与惯性测量并不共享同一时钟，强行离散化会牺牲精度并引入延迟。本工作基于钳制非均匀 B 样条构建连续时间因子图，使各类测量都能按原始时间戳融合，节点分布自适应运动剧烈程度，并在线估计机间时钟偏移——数百毫秒的初始时偏可在 3 s 内收敛至亚毫秒级。同时提出钳制 B 样条的闭式扩展与删除算法，使控制点增删不改变已有曲线形状；并提出可证明收敛的增量异步块坐标下降并行求解策略，实时性足以直接用于无人机控制回路。真机实验达到 0.046 m / 1.8° RMSE，在高速序列上较离散时间优化最高降低 60% 误差。

Highlights 技术亮点

  • Continuous-time factor graph. Clamped non-uniform B-splines fuse asynchronous, multi-rate bearing / distance / IMU measurements at their raw timestamps, adapt to the aggressiveness of the motion and remove the latency inherent to discrete-time formulations.
  • Online clock alignment. The inter-robot clock offset is estimated jointly; an initial offset of hundreds of milliseconds converges to sub-millisecond accuracy within 3 seconds.
  • Shape-preserving spline surgery. Closed-form extension and deletion of clamped B-splines lets control points be added or removed without deforming the curve, which improves both efficiency and consistency.
  • Parallel solver with a convergence proof. An incremental asynchronous block coordinate descent scheme keeps the estimator real-time enough to be used directly for UAV control.
  • Real-robot accuracy. 0.046 m / 1.8° RMSE, and up to 60% lower error than discrete-time optimization on high-speed sequences.
  • 连续时间因子图。基于钳制非均匀 B 样条，使异步、异频的方位/距离/IMU 测量按原始时间戳融合，自适应运动剧烈程度并消除离散时间建模固有的延迟。
  • 在线时钟对齐。联合在线估计机间时钟偏移，数百毫秒初始时偏可在 3 s 内收敛至亚毫秒级。
  • 保形的样条增删。提出钳制 B 样条的闭式扩展与删除算法，控制点增删不改变曲线形状，同时提升优化效率与一致性。
  • 可证明收敛的并行求解。增量异步块坐标下降策略提升实时性，可直接用于无人机控制等场景。
  • 真机精度。真机实验 0.046 m / 1.8° RMSE，高速序列较离散时间优化最高降低 60%。
