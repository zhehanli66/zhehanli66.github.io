Abstract 摘要

This demonstration takes the lab's relative localization stack out of the lab. I was responsible for integrating the heterogeneous air-ground platform, for the real-robot bring-up and for the on-site deployment. Built on our own CREPES relative localization hardware and the CoNi-MPC control algorithm, the UAV tracks and manoeuvres with a tracked ground vehicle without GPS and without any global map, using only the relative observations between the two robots. At the IROS 2025 EXPO the system ran as a public live demo: visitors teleoperated the ground vehicle themselves while the UAV followed it fully autonomously.

这次演示把实验室的相对定位技术栈带出了实验室。我负责异构空地平台集成、真机联调与现场部署。系统基于自研 CREPES 相对定位硬件与 CoNi-MPC 控制算法，在无 GPS、无全局地图的条件下，仅依靠两台机器人之间的相对观测，实现无人机对履带车的自主跟踪与协同机动。在 IROS 2025 EXPO 上，该系统作为公开现场演示运行：观众实时遥控地面车，无人机全程自主跟随。

Highlights 技术亮点

  • No GPS, no global map. The UAV relies purely on inter-robot relative observations from the in-house CREPES hardware.
  • Heterogeneous platform integration. Responsible for putting the air and ground platforms together, for real-robot bring-up and for the on-site deployment.
  • Public live demonstration. At the IROS 2025 EXPO the audience teleoperated the ground vehicle while the UAV followed autonomously throughout.
  • 无 GPS、无全局地图。无人机完全依靠自研 CREPES 硬件提供的机间相对观测工作。
  • 异构平台集成。负责空地平台集成、真机联调与现场部署。
  • 公开现场演示。IROS 2025 EXPO 现场由观众实时遥控地面车，无人机全程自主跟随。
