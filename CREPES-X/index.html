CREPES-X: Hierarchical Bearing-Distance-Inertial Direct Cooperative Relative Pose Estimation System CREPES-X：多层次直接式的协作多机器人相对位姿估计系统

Abstract 摘要

CREPES-X targets cooperative localization where it is hardest: GPS-denied environments, non-line-of-sight conditions and fast relative motion. The system couples custom hardware with a hierarchical estimator. On the hardware side, an infrared fisheye camera, UWB and an IMU are integrated into a single module, together with the infrared LED detection, tracking and coding / decoding algorithms that turn the camera into an identity-aware bearing sensor; the module is 77% smaller and 50% lighter than its predecessor. On the estimation side, a single-frame closed-form initialization is refined by a multi-frame optimization that tightly couples the IMU pre-integration of all robots, and neither a global pose nor a shared map is required. An efficient bearing outlier rejection based on angular consistency keeps 96.8% precision even when 90% of the observations are outliers. Real-world experiments reach 0.073 m / 1.8° RMSE and run in real time with 20 robots.

CREPES-X 面向协同定位中最困难的场景：GPS 拒止、非视距以及高速相对运动。系统由自研硬件与多层次估计器共同构成。硬件方面，将红外鱼眼相机、UWB 与 IMU 集成为一体化模块，并配套红外 LED 的识别、跟踪与编解码算法，使相机成为带身份信息的方位传感器；相较前代硬件体积缩小 77%、重量降低 50%。估计方面，先用单帧闭式初始化给出初值，再由多帧优化紧耦合融合多机 IMU 预积分，全过程不依赖全局位姿或共享地图。同时提出基于角度一致性的高效方位外点剔除算法，在 90% 外点率下仍保持 96.8% 的精确率。真机实验达到 0.073 m / 1.8° RMSE，并支持 20 台机器人实时运行。

Highlights 技术亮点

  • An integrated relative localization module. Infrared fisheye camera, UWB and IMU in one device, with the LED detection / tracking and coding / decoding stack that makes bearings identity-aware — 77% smaller and 50% lighter than the previous generation.
  • Direct estimation, no global frame. A single-frame closed-form initialization followed by a multi-frame optimization that tightly fuses the IMU pre-integration of every robot, without relying on global poses or a shared map.
  • Robust to extreme outlier ratios. An efficient angular-consistency test rejects bearing outliers and holds 96.8% precision at a 90% outlier ratio.
  • Validated on hardware. 0.073 m / 1.8° RMSE in real-world experiments, running in real time with 20 robots.
  • 一体化相对定位硬件。红外鱼眼相机 / UWB / IMU 一体化设计，配套红外 LED 识别跟踪与编解码算法使方位观测自带身份信息，较前代体积缩小 77%、重量降低 50%。
  • 直接式估计，无需全局坐标系。单帧闭式初始化配合多帧优化，紧耦合融合多机 IMU 预积分，不依赖全局位姿或共享地图。
  • 极端外点率下依然稳健。基于角度一致性的高效方位外点剔除算法，在 90% 外点率下保持 96.8% 精确率。
  • 真机验证。真机实验 0.073 m / 1.8° RMSE，支持 20 台机器人实时运行。
